# Interview Day Checklist

## Night before (45 min max, then sleep)
- [ ] Read [One-Page-Recall.md](One-Page-Recall.md) once, slowly.
- [ ] Re-read your **bug log** in PROGRESS.md ("Timed coding log"). Those are *your* failure modes.
- [ ] Say your 90-second intro out loud twice (08-Lead, §2).
- [ ] Say your top project story out loud once, with numbers.
- [ ] Type from memory: reverse list, LRU, BFS grid, blocking queue (10 min total, no checking).
- [ ] Prepare the setup: laptop charged, headphones, a quiet room, a blank editor with **no autocomplete** open, water, and paper + pen for drawing.
- [ ] Stop studying by the planned time. Sleep beats one more topic.

## 30 minutes before
- [ ] Skim the Pattern triggers table + C++ must-says (Recall sheet).
- [ ] Warm up: solve one **easy** problem out loud (e.g. valid parentheses) to get your voice and fingers going.
- [ ] Open a scratch file for notes; test your camera, mic and screen-share.

## During
- [ ] Clarify before coding: input size, duplicates, negatives, empty input, return format.
- [ ] Say the brute force, then the better idea + **invariant** + complexity. Ask *"Shall I code this?"*
- [ ] Narrate lightly while coding; name things clearly; extract helpers.
- [ ] **Dry-run** one example line by line before saying "done". Check the edge cases.
- [ ] If stuck for over 2 minutes, say what you're thinking and ask a clarifying question. Interviewers give hints to people who communicate.
- [ ] For theory: 30-second answer first, then offer depth ("I can go into how the control block works if useful").
- [ ] For experience questions: STAR, **"I" not "we"** for your part, and numbers.
- [ ] Don't bluff on Vivado internals. Say *"I haven't worked on that directly; here's how I'd reason about it…"*, which is a strong signal.
- [ ] Guard confidentiality: no Synopsys proprietary details or customer names beyond what's public on your resume.

## After (same day, 15 min)
- [ ] Write down every question asked, and where you hesitated → add them to PROGRESS.md.
- [ ] Update the flashcards for next rounds.
- [ ] Send a short thank-you via the recruiter if appropriate.
