    The model in five sentences
    
      
      
        
    1. A combinational netlist is a DAG: pins are nodes, timing arcs are weighted edges. Registers cut every loop.
      2. 
        
    2. Levelize with Kahn: a node is ready once all its inputs are done. Leftover nodes mean a combinational loop (find it with SCC).
      3. 
        
    3. Arrival = the latest a signal can get here: max over inputs of (input arrival + delay). That's longest path, which is DP in topological order.
      4. 
        
    4. Required = the latest a signal may leave and still reach every output by T: min over outputs of (output required − delay), in reverse order.
      5. 
        
    5. Slack = required − arrival. Negative means a timing violation; the critical path is the predecessor trail of the worst output.
